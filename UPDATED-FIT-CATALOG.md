# Se7en fit catalog update

This folder is the updated website package based on the uploaded `se7en-black` site.

## Catalog changes

- The original Box Fit, Oversize Fit, Regular, Core, and Contour items are preserved.
- The uploaded products are added as separate Box Fit and Oversize Fit New Drop items in `assets/box-fit/` and `assets/oversize-fit/`.
- When a color has two uploaded shirt images, both are kept as a front/back gallery on that color option.
- Box Fit sizes: M, L, XL, XXL.
- Oversize Fit sizes: S, M, L, XL, XXL.
- The new-drop product pages use the uploaded chart images and matching measurements.
- Original product IDs and original product images remain available.
